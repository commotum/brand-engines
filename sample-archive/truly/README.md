## No One Is Just One Flavor

**You’re allowed more than one favorite—and more than one version of yourself.** Your mood changes. Your plans change. Your drink can keep up. These headlines make variety personal: impossible coins to flip, jackets with too many pockets, and permission to enjoy what you actually like instead of what looks impressive. Choosing a flavor becomes a way to explore, express yourself, or refuse to choose at all. The focus is the person holding the can and the many different ways they can be Truly.

## Good Times Don’t Wait

**The party doesn’t need a perfect moment. It needs a starting point.** A porch becomes a destination. A tailgate refuses to end. An ordinary afternoon develops a story nobody is going to stop telling. These headlines celebrate the occasion and everything it sets in motion: spontaneous invitations, improbable guests, endless summer, and friends making questionable plans together. Loud or laid-back, the good time starts wherever you are. The focus is the moment, its momentum, and the memories that outlast the can.

## Naturally Ridiculous

**Fruit had bigger plans than the fruit bowl.** Watermelon and kiwi fall in love. Lemon gets more zest than a Zumba instructor. Flavors come together in combinations the universe apparently never approved. These headlines give the drink a life of its own: ingredients become characters, refreshment becomes an impossible sensation, and the meeting of fruit and alcohol gets gleefully strange. The focus is the product’s personality and sensory payoff—sweetness, fizz, flavor, and booze turned into something far beyond an ordinary refreshment.
